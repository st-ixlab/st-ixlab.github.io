Our papers were accepted to ACM CHI 2026 as Posters! 

- "Investigating the Impact of Lying Postures on Mixed Reality Interaction" by Yujin Jeong, Jiwon Lee, Jae-Yeop Jeong, Euna Byun, HyeokJu Ko

- "How Video See-Through MR Affects Pointing on Physical Devices: From Smartphones to Large Monitors" by Yujin Jeong, Jae-Yeop Jeong, HyeokJu Ko

- "Knight Path: Supporting Player Sense-making in Game Narratives via Visual Scaffolding and Spoiler-Aware AI" by Euna Byun, Jae-Yeop Jeong, Yewon Min

- "Reading the Mood Behind Words: Integrating Prosody-Derived Emotional Context into Socially Responsive VR Agents" Cowork with SangYeop Jeong, YeongSeo Na, Seung Gyu Jeong, Seoung-Eun Kim

Congratulations to all authors!
