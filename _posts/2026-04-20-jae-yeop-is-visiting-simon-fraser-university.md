Jae-Yeop Jeong will visit the Virtual/Augmented/Extended Reality, Visual/Immersive Analytics, Interaction, Systems, and Experiments (VVISE) Lab led by Wolfgang Stuerzlinger at Simon Fraser University in Vancouver, Canada.

From April 29 to August 16, Jae-Yeop will be conducting a collaborative research project on user performance modeling in 2D/3D interfaces.

Congratulations and good luck!
